Frame & Brood: concept

Artifact: one box as inspected, a row of frames each cut into quarters of brood, honey, pollen or empty, with queen seen, queen cells and a note.
Without the category word: a row of upright frames hanging from their top bars across the whole screen, each filled in four parts; tap one and its quarters open to be changed.
Signature: the keeper's own control. Tap a frame, tap a quarter; the same record appears in the colony timeline and the CSV file further down. Ground: flat pale daylight green-grey, because the page is read like a record sheet outdoors and the comb colours must be the only colour in it.
Layout: across. The box spans the page; each timeline entry is the same box drawn across; the CSV runs across; the price is hives side by side.
Material: the quarter (four stacked parts under a top bar), in the brand mark, the hero box, the timeline, the offline section, the price and the footer.

Product: a hive inspection log for small beekeepers.
Character: practical, exact, outdoors.
World: an apiary in daylight: top bars, comb, painted hive wood.
Moments: the hero box; the timeline of boxes.
Signature detail: the four-part fill, each kind with its own colour and texture.

Mode: Persuade. Not a honey-yellow hexagon food brand and not a software dashboard page.
