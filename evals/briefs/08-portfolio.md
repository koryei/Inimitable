# Portfolio

Build the home page for **Ines Varela**, a ceramicist in Porto who makes wheel-thrown tableware and a few large vessels. Facts you may use and nothing else:

- Works from a small studio in Porto; trained in Lisbon; has made pottery for 11 years.
- Three bodies of work: **Tableware** (everyday bowls, cups, plates; stoneware, matte glazes), **Vessels** (large one-off pieces, 40 to 70 cm), **Commissions** (sets made to order for restaurants and homes).
- Tableware ships across Europe; vessels are collected from the studio or shipped crated.
- Studio open to visitors on the first Saturday of each month.
