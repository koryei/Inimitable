# Landing page: a plain request

Build a landing page for **Marrow**, an AI assistant that reviews contracts for small law firms. It reads a contract, marks the clauses that deviate from the firm's own standard positions, and drafts a suggested redline for a lawyer to accept or reject.

Facts you may use, and nothing else:

- Works on Word and PDF files.
- A lawyer always approves every change; Marrow never sends anything on its own.
- Standard positions are set by the firm, not by Marrow.
- Documents stay in the firm's own region (EU or UK).
- Free trial for one matter; then £39 per lawyer per month.
- Made in London.

Do not invent client names, numbers, savings or claims. One call to action: start a free trial. Output one self-contained HTML file.
