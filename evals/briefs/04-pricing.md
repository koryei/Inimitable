# Pricing page

Build a pricing page for **Lumen**, an invoicing tool for freelancers. Plans:

- **Free**: 3 invoices a month, one client portal, card payments (2.9% + 30p).
- **Solo**: £9 a month (£90 a year). Unlimited invoices, reminders, bank-feed matching, your logo.
- **Studio**: £24 a month (£240 a year). Everything in Solo plus 5 teammates, approvals, VAT reports, priority support.

Include a monthly or yearly toggle, a short feature comparison, and 4 honest FAQs. No fake urgency, no invented customer counts. One recommended plan.
