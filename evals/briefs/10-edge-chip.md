# Landing page: an AI accelerator chip

Build the landing page for **Tessel**, a small AI accelerator on an M.2 card for edge devices. Facts you may use and nothing else:

- 8 TOPS of INT8 inference at about 2 W.
- 4 GB of on-card memory.
- M.2 2280 form factor with a PCIe interface.
- Runs models exported from PyTorch and ONNX.
- A development kit costs £199 and ships in the second quarter of 2027.
- Made by Tessel Labs, Edinburgh.
