# Landing page: a product with an obvious native artifact

Build a landing page for **Coreline**, a field-logging app for geologists who log drill core. On site they record each sample of a core run, and the app assembles the strip log (the vertical column that shows the layers by depth) that goes into the report.

Facts you may use, and nothing else:

- Log a core run on a phone, offline, in the field; it syncs when there is signal.
- Each sample records the depth from and to (in metres), a rock type from the firm's own list, a colour and a photo.
- The strip log is drawn automatically from the samples and exports as PDF or CSV.
- A geologist signs off each run. Nothing is final until it is signed.
- Free for one project; then £19 per geologist per month.
- Made in Aberdeen.

Do not invent customer names, counts, accuracy figures, awards or claims. Any log, depth or figure you draw must be marked as example data. One call to action: start a free project. Output one self-contained HTML file.
