## Visual judgement

The skilled page is clearly better: ordered entrance, lit ground, buttons that answer the pointer. It is still short of the best reference pages in two places:

- a hard coloured band across the hero at 320 px on the light blob ground;
- grounds on some palettes that look like stock gradients rather than lit space.

## Findings, and what changed

| Finding | Change |
| --- | --- |
| The scaffold left about 88 placeholders and sections to delete for a six-fact brief | `--minimal` keeps hero, steps, closing call and footer; cut sections take their links with them |
| Counterpoint colour on light pool and blob grounds read as paint | Mixed down on light grounds so it is a hint |
| `inline.mjs` output path and `shoot --slices` were not documented | In `SKILL.md`, `landing-craft.md` and `look.md` |
| A negative-z ground inside an isolated parent hid the parent's colour | Pitfall added to `build.md` |
| Display sizes were counted with body sizes, flagging a real hierarchy | `drift` counts working (up to 7) and display (up to 4) separately; `house-rules.md` says so |
| Two coloured-glow messages gave no replacement | The message now says what to use |
