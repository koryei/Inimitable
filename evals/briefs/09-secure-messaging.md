# Landing page: encrypted messaging

Build the landing page for **Cipherline**, an end-to-end encrypted messaging app for journalists and activists. Facts you may use and nothing else:

- Messages are encrypted on the device; the service cannot read them.
- It keeps no message content and no contact lists on its servers.
- Messages can disappear after a time the sender chooses.
- Works on iPhone, Android and desktop.
- Free for individuals; a Team plan is £5 per person per month with shared channels and an admin console.
- Open protocol; independent security audits are planned but none have been published yet.

Do not invent user counts, certifications, audit results, speeds or other numbers. Any readout or figure you draw must be marked as an example. One primary action: get the app.
