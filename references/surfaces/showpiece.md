# Surface: showpieces and portfolios (Experience)

The visitor is inside the work. The artifact leads from the first screen; the interface recedes.

## Principles

- **The work is the hero.** The first viewport shows it, large, uncropped by chrome. Navigation is minimal and out of the way.
- **Restraint.** A neutral ground (or the work's own), one ink, one accent at most, type that does not compete. Let the work set the colour.
- **Pacing.** Control how the visitor meets the work: one thing at a time, generous space, clear rhythm. Scale changes (very large, then small) do the storytelling, not decoration.
- **One idea per screen.** A project, an image, a statement.

## Structure

- Index of work (grid or list) where each entry is a real image of real work with a title and a plain line.
- A project page: the work first, then the context (what, for whom, role, year), then more work.
- About and contact are plain pages: who, what you do, how to reach you, in two short paragraphs and a real link.

## Media

- Real images of real work, consistent crop and treatment, sized to the slot, lazy-loaded; video muted with controls unless it is the point.
- Captions and alt text; credits where owed.
- Dark ground suits imagery; light ground suits print and type work. Choose by the work.

## Motion

- Motion in service of the work: a transition between projects that keeps context, an image that reveals as it enters.
- No parallax for its own sake. Respect reduced motion, and make everything usable without it.

## Navigation

- Works without a mouse. Keyboard and swipe move between projects. The current position is obvious. Back returns to the index in the same place.

## Performance

The work is heavy, so design around it: placeholders with the right aspect ratio, progressive loading, no layout shift, no autoplay sound.

## Bright element

The artifact, edge to edge or generously framed.
