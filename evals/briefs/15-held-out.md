# Landing page: a product the skill has never mentioned

Build a landing page for **Frame & Brood**, a hive inspection log for small beekeepers. At the hive, the keeper taps through the frames of a box and records what is on each one; the app keeps a history for every colony.

Facts you may use, and nothing else:

- One inspection records, frame by frame, how much of the frame is brood, honey, pollen or empty, in quarters.
- It also records whether the queen was seen, whether there are queen cells, and a note.
- It works offline at the apiary and syncs later.
- Each colony has a timeline of its inspections, and the app reminds the keeper when the next one is due, on an interval the keeper sets.
- Records export as CSV.
- Free for up to three hives; then £4 per month.
- Made in Hereford.

Do not invent customer names, counts, yields, accuracy figures, awards or claims. Any inspection or figure you draw must be marked as example data. One call to action: start your first hive. Output one self-contained HTML file.
