How it works

Availability in, rota out.

Staff enter their availability first, then you build the week from it.

1

Staff say when they can work

Everyone enters the times they can and cannot work.

2

You drag shifts onto the week

The rota is a weekly grid. Drop a shift on a day and it is placed.

3

Tern flags what breaks a rule

It warns when someone is double-booked, or would go over the hours agreed in their contract.

On phones

Their shifts on their phone. Every swap through you.

Staff see their own shifts on their phone and can ask to swap. You approve every swap, so the rota only changes when you say.
