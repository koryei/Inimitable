# Landing page: a dull product

Build a landing page for **Tern**, a shift-scheduling app for small cafes and shops. The manager builds the week as a grid of shifts, staff see their own shifts on their phones, and it warns when the rota breaks a rule.

Facts you may use, and nothing else:

- Staff enter when they can and cannot work; the manager drags shifts onto a weekly grid.
- It warns when someone is double-booked, or would go over the hours agreed in their contract.
- Staff see their shifts on their phone and can ask to swap; the manager approves every swap.
- Free for up to 10 staff; then £2 per person per month.
- Made in Manchester.

Do not invent customer names, counts, savings, ratings or claims. Any rota or figure you draw must be marked as example data. One call to action: try it free. Output one self-contained HTML file.
