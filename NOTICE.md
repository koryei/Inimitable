Inimitable is licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`). It builds on the ideas of three earlier design skills. This file says exactly how, so that credit goes where it is owed.

## unslop

**unslop** was created by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir), [heydamir.design/unslop](https://heydamir.design/unslop)) and is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Inimitable adapts material from it:

- the nine-layer repair order (spacing, colour, icons, copy, type, layout, details, visuals, motion)
- the list of signs of machine-made UI (`references/anti-slop.md`)
- the idea of one bright element per screen
- parts of the starting token values (spacing scale, neutral ramps, type scale, radii, motion durations)

Changes made: reorganised into reference files, reworded, merged with rules from the other sources and from our own testing, extended to terminal UI and native mobile, and made subordinate to a project's own design rules. The adapted files carry this credit in their footer. Anyone who copies or adapts them must keep the credit, as the licence asks.

## impeccable

**impeccable** ([impeccable.style](https://impeccable.style)) is a design skill with four modes (persuade, operate, read, experience), a brief-first way of working and a long list of design reviews. Inimitable borrows the idea of choosing a mode from the surface being built, and the idea that a product's own records of its design come before the skill's taste. The installed copy has no licence file, so nothing is copied. Everything here is restated in our own words.

## tasteskill

**tasteskill** ([tasteskill.dev](https://tasteskill.dev)), by Leonxlnx, is a design skill for building websites. Inimitable borrows the ideas of establishing what is real before planning, of listing what belongs to this client and no competitor, of building around one idea, and of a finishing gate that checks in a real browser. The upstream project is MIT licensed. Even so, nothing is copied here: everything is restated in our own words.
