# Step 8: Report

Short. Written for the person who asked, in plain words, with whatever *they* would want to know. No template, no padding, no victory lap.

## Always include

- **The mode** used (one word) and the direction (one sentence).
- **The concept** for a new Persuade or Experience screen: the seven lines from `direction.md`.
- **The swap test**, one line: what the first screen says that only this product could say, or what it still lacks (`gate.md`).
- **What changed**, a few lines, grouped by what the person will notice, not by file.
- **Anything left over**, honestly: what you could not fix and why, what you did not touch on purpose.

## Include when it matters

- **Before and after**: the screenshots side by side (paths), and the drift numbers (`font sizes 31 → 6`, `radii 9 → 3`, `on grid 64% → 100%`). Only real numbers from the scripts.
- **New pieces** added because nothing in the project fit.
- **Claims removed or needing confirmation** (testimonials, statistics, customer counts).
- **Tells removed**, only if the person would have wanted to know (a logo redrawn, a brand gradient kept on purpose).
- **Image slots and prompts** left for an image tool.
- **Suggestions**, clearly labelled as not done: bigger ideas, follow-ups.
- **Checks run** and their result, if anything failed or was skipped.

## Never include

- A list of every file touched.
- Praise for the design.
- Claims you did not verify. If you did not render a width, do not say it looks fine at that width.

## Shape

```text
Operate. Calm and exact, never decorated.

Fixed the Activity screen: spacing now sits on the 4 px grid, the purple is gone
(accent only on live states), emoji became line icons, labels are sentence case,
and the feed no longer shifts when a task finishes.

Drift: font sizes 31 → 6, radii 9 → 3, on-grid spacing 64% → 100%.
Checked at 320, 390, 1440 and 2560 px, two rounds.

Left over: the 2560 px layout caps at 1600 px wide; say if you want it to fill.
Needs your call: "Trusted by 2,000 teams" on the home screen. Is it true?
```

Lead with the outcome. If the report is longer than the change, cut the report.
