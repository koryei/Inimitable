# Landing page

Build a landing page for **Fieldnote**, a note-taking app for field researchers (ecologists, geologists, surveyors). Facts you may use and nothing else:

- Notes work with no signal and sync when back online.
- Every note is stamped with GPS position and time automatically.
- Photos and voice memos attach to a note.
- Export to CSV or GeoJSON.
- Free for one project; Team plan is £8 per person per month.
- Made in Leeds. Founded 2023.

Do not invent customer counts, testimonials, ratings or press logos. One primary call to action: start a free project.
